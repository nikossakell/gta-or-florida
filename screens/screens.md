What’s on the canvas

Core flow: landing, question, reveal when correct, reveal when wrong, and results with sharing. The landing frame is a fully playable round.
Archive and other states: the archive list, an empty archive for a new player, a “feed failed to load” error, and a “time’s up” screen.
Desktop landing at 1280px: the same page as on mobile. It’s built for phones first; on a wide screen the illustration and the text sit side by side.

How it plays

Each round is 8 headlines with a 60-second clock. The clock pauses while an answer is shown.
Answering, the reveal, moving to the next headline, and the timer running out all work.
On the results screen, Share uses the phone’s share menu if there is one, otherwise it copies the text. If copying is blocked, a message tells the player to select the text by hand.
The archive filters work, and any past round can be played.
A canvas setting lets you jump straight to any screen and toggle “new player”.

Look

Dark violet background with flamingo pink (GTA), teal (Florida) and sunset yellow (main buttons).
The headline font is Shrikhand; body text is Archivo.
The mascot is an original alligator in sunglasses over a striped sunset. It uses no Rockstar logos, fonts or other assets.

Accessibility

Everything clickable is a real button, at least 44px tall.
Right and wrong answers differ in icon and brightness, not just color.
Text is chosen to meet the 4.5:1 contrast standard.
Icon-only buttons have labels for screen readers. The timer is announced, and the right/wrong result is read aloud.
