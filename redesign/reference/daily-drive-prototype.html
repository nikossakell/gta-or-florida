DAILYDRIVE

Five headlines, sixty seconds. Is each one real Florida news or a GTA mission? Right calls keep you on the highway to Vice. Wrong ones take the scenic route.

Name's Gus. I drive, you guess. Mind the gators. And the clock.
  1. 1Read the headline. Long ones stay readable.
  2. 2Hit G for GTA or F for Florida.
  3. 3Reach Vice, then share your postcard.
